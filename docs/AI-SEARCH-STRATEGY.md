# AI Search Strategy

Brief notes for Phase 3 programmatic content and Phase 3 Batch F polish.

---

## Answer-first content

Every indexable country, city, guide, and blog article carries a **QuickAnswer** field — a direct, factual paragraph at the top of the page that answers the primary query without preamble. Templates render QuickAnswer before body sections so AI crawlers and featured snippets get a clean extract.

---

## Entity consistency

Company facts live in `src/config/site.ts`. **`site.canonicalDescription`** (set in Batch F) feeds Organization schema, `public/llms.txt`, and cross-page entity references. Never hardcode company names, locations, or claims in page copy.

---

## llms.txt

Machine-readable site summary at **`/llms.txt`** (static file in `public/`, generated by `npm run generate:llms` from `src/lib/llms.ts`).

Contents: two-sentence company blurb plus categorised URLs for products, solutions, export markets, cities, guides, comparisons, blog, and resources. Target under 200 lines.

Regenerate after content changes that add or remove live URLs.

---

## Crawler policy

`robots.ts` allows:

- `*` — all user agents on `/`, disallow `/api/` and `/_next/`
- **GPTBot**, **ClaudeBot**, **PerplexityBot**, **Google-Extended**, **CCBot**, **Bingbot** — each explicitly allowed on `/`, disallow `/api/`

Draft pages (`draft: true`) emit `noindex`. No paywall or bot-blocking on export or product content.

Sitemap index: `/sitemap-index.xml`.

---

## Schema constraints on export

Export country and city pages must **not** emit:

- `LocalBusiness` (we export from India; no local storefront per market)
- `AggregateRating` or `Review` (no unverified ratings)
- `Offer` with price (quotes are enquiry-based, not listed prices)

Enforced by `npm run audit:schema` after build.

---

## Blog and guides for AI queries

Blog articles target buyer questions (spec mistakes, export delays, quotation reading) with question-led H2s. Link to live guides and comparisons for depth. See `docs/KEYWORD-MAP.md` for AI query column on blog URLs.

Editorial pipeline: `docs/EDITORIAL-CALENDAR.md` (24 topics planned).
